Control Pause (CP)After a normal out-breath, hold until the first definite urge to breathe.
Maximum Pause (MP)Hold past the first urge to a moderate, comfortable limit.
CP then MPOne hold. Tap at the first urge to log the CP, the timer keeps going, tap again to finish the MP.
RecoveryAfter an MP, gentle shallow breathing until your breathing settles. Tap Recovered when it has. This should get shorter with practice.
Very Shallow Breathing (VSB)Very rapid, very shallow breathing for 3 to 5 minutes, counted down.
Normalised Shallow Breathing (NSB)Breathing settles back to gentle shallow breaths. The timer counts up until you press Done.
During the MPWalking steps, energetic steps, belly pumps, lying on your left side or sitting. After the hold, while you recover and breathe, pick which you did and enter the count where there is one.
Pulse checkA 5 second countdown, then 30 seconds to count your heartbeats. The app doubles it to beats per minute.
