Buteyko Practice privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

What the app stores

Your practice history: the date and time of each session, your Control Pause and Maximum Pause times, and any pulse readings and notes you choose to add.

Where it is stored

  • In your browser on each device you use, so the app works offline.
  • If you sign in with Google, in one file called "Buteyko Practice log.json" inside a "Buteyko Practice" folder in your own Google Drive.

There is no Buteyko Practice server. Your history is never sent to the app's developer or anyone else, and the developer cannot see it.

Google access

The app asks Google for the "drive.file" permission only. That lets it see and change files it created itself, and nothing else in your Drive. It also reads your Google account email address to show which account you're syncing to. Use of this information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising or cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Removing your data

  • To remove sessions, delete them in the app's History view.
  • To remove everything from Google Drive, delete the "Buteyko Practice" folder from your Drive.
  • To stop the app accessing your Drive, sign out in the app, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To clear the copy on a device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Health information

The app is a practice timer and diary. It does not give medical advice. Talk to your Buteyko teacher or doctor about your breathing practice.
